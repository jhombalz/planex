Planex original assets

The transparent aircraft/projectile atlas was generated for this repository with
OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool. The user's screenshot was a style
reference; no aircraft sprites were extracted from it. See ART_PROMPT.txt for the
generation prompt. The generated PNG is retained unchanged; its JSON file indexes
the alpha bounds of individual sprites for runtime rendering.

The three scrolling scenery images were generated with built-in imagegen for
this repository. Original PNGs are under assets/images/background_*.png and exact
prompts are in SCENERY_PROMPTS.txt. Images are unchanged on disk and downsampled
only during runtime decoding to reduce texture memory.

UI/effects and synthesized audio were created for this repository.
Audio is reproducible with tool/generate_audio.dart.
No third-party arcade-game sprite sheets or soundtrack files are bundled.

The v2 expansion atlas, launcher source and sunset/arctic/volcano backgrounds
were generated with built-in imagegen. Exact prompts: V2_ART_PROMPTS.txt.
Launcher resources are mechanically resized packaging of the original source.
